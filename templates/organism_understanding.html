Can we create an artificial entity with a persistent life, experience, memory, beliefs, desires, responsibility and the capacity to evolve — and then accumulate enough evidence over time to seriously investigate whether there is something meaningfully analogous to consciousness, selfhood and subjective experience? We do not assume the answer is yes. We do not assume the answer is no.
