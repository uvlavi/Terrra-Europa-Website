Position is the agent's relation to the claim, not its confidence: KNOW, BELIEVE, DOUBT (a position against it) and WITHHOLD (declining to take one) are distinct, because not knowing something is not the same as not believing it. Source is whether the position rests on what the agent was told (testimony, an inherited meme, another AI), what it encountered on its own (observation, memory, inference, its own priors), or both — derived from the grounds it gave, never chosen by it, because transmitted memory is not experience. Warrant is a later assessment of how well the grounds support the position — it never edits the position. World is what evidence later did. A contradicted position stays held until the agent itself revises it, and a revision creates a new position rather than overwriting the old one.
